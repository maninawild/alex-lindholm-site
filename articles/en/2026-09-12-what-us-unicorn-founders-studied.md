Almost thirty percent of U.S. unicorn founders studied just one major: Computer Science.

An analysis of over two thousand founders behind roughly eight hundred unicorns founded between 2010 and 2024 shows a heavy concentration in technical roots.

The top three undergraduate paths:

- Computer Science at 29 percent.
- Economics, Business, and Finance at 23.8 percent.
- Engineering excluding CS at 11.5 percent.

Combine CS, Engineering, Sciences, and Math, and STEM fields account for 57.3 percent of the total.

That first wave makes total sense for the SaaS, mobile, and AI era. Having a cofounder who could ship the first version pre-funding was a massive speed advantage.

Yet the remaining 42.7 percent came from outside STEM. Business majors formed the second largest group, proving that industry insight in fintech or insurance matters just as much as writing the backend.

Nearly one in five studied psychology, history, art, or design - fields most assume have nothing to do with building billion-dollar companies.
