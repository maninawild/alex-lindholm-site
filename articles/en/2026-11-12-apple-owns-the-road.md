Apple may have found the funniest way to win the AI race: by refusing to behave like everyone else.

While Amazon, Microsoft, Google and Meta are pouring absurd amounts of money into GPUs and data centres, Apple is staying relatively light.

No desperate model race. No panic-buying GPUs. No $100B infrastructure flex.

Instead, they partner with Google, keep building their own silicon, use Private Cloud Compute where needed, and let other companies burn the money.

The smartest part is Siri.

With iOS 27, Apple is turning it into a gateway for external models like Claude, Gemini, ChatGPT and Grok.

Which means Apple does not need to build the best model.

It owns the device, the OS, the interface and more than 2.5 billion active devices.

Everyone else is fighting over who builds the engine.

Apple owns the road. Since the Romans, we have known: own the road, own the empire.
