Anthropic launched Claude Commerce Agents, turning Claude into an AI operating layer for online retailers.

It handles practical commerce workflows out of the box:

- Turns natural language into personalized carts.
- Answers detailed shipping and availability questions.
- Drafts pricing, inventory, and promotion updates.
- Integrates directly with Shopify infrastructure.
- Keeps humans in the loop before transactions execute.

The early metrics look sharp: larger carts and higher conversion rates.

Yet the most interesting design choices are here:

No new checkout protocol. No proprietary marketplace. No ad network.

Instead of building another walled garden where consumers have to shop, Anthropic is positioning Claude as the reasoning layer for existing infrastructure.

While OpenAI and Google try to pull users into their own consumer apps, Anthropic is betting that independent retailers want an AI brain running on their own properties.

Own the intelligence. Let everyone else own the rails.
