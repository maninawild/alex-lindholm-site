Stockholm Mafia 🇸🇪

Some of the most exciting AI startups in Europe are quietly emerging out of Stockholm.

Over the past decade, the city has produced world-class operators from companies like Lovable, Spotify, Legora, and Klarna — teams that have scaled products used by millions worldwide.

Now, many of those operators are stepping out to build their own ventures.
