Skip one Netflix episode tonight and watch this instead.

Stanford has a very good lecture on Self-Improving AI Agents, and it is one of the clearest explanations I’ve seen of how we move from static chatbots to systems that actually learn from execution.

It covers test-time compute, verifiers, code feedback, multi-step planning, reinforcement learning, deep research agents, long-horizon evaluation and the loops that let agents improve from previous runs.

The useful part is that it connects ideas that are usually scattered across papers into one coherent picture.

If you are building agents, this is probably worth more than another evening of random AI demos.
