Amazon signed a $50 billion investment into OpenAI.

This marks the single largest investment in artificial intelligence history.

With this $50B injection securing roughly a 5% stake, OpenAI remains the most valuable AI lab on earth, and Amazon now joins Microsoft as a core stakeholder. This massive capital will directly fuel the development and training of next-generation models.

Big Tech has officially moved past the experimentation phase. They are aggressively securing their positions before the window of opportunity closes. The race to own the AI infrastructure layer is consolidating much faster than anticipated.

The big question now: Who stands to lose the most when both Amazon and Microsoft hold massive stakes in OpenAI?
