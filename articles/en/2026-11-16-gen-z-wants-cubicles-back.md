Apparently Gen Z wants cubicles back.

Honestly, I understand them.

We spent years tearing down walls in the name of “collaboration” and ended up with offices where everyone hears every call, every conversation and every Slack notification from three desks away.

Then came hot-desking, so you also lost the small luxury of having your own corner. Guys, hot-desking means you are poor. Your venture has no money for a real human office, sorry.

A younger employee recently wrote on Reddit: “I wish I had office cubicles like my parents did.”

Ten years ago that sentence would have sounded insane. Now a grey fabric partition almost looks premium.

I don’t think people miss cubicles. They miss control over their environment, a place to concentrate. A door for a private call. Somewhere you can work for two hours without participating in the entire office by accident.

We probably did not need to destroy the old office. We needed to fix it.

Quiet rooms for actual work, open areas for collaboration, and enough personal space that people do not need noise-cancelling headphones just to survive Tuesday.

Which office trend do you think we will quietly reverse next?
