“I haven’t made it yet.”

Every single founder I talk to says this. Almost none of them are right.

You simply set the bar unreasonably high — which is what founders do. You maintain towering expectations for yourself, your team, and your investors.

Stop measuring where you are today against your ultimate, distant destination. Instead, measure it against where you started:

- Your very first customer.
- Your first working pilot.
- That terrifying first investor call.

That is real, tangible progress.

Billions of people never take the leap to build a business. You did. And right now, you are juggling ten different critical roles at once.

I know most founders trap themselves with impossibly short deadlines, but nobody builds something enduring overnight. It requires sustained time, patience, and the discipline to show up and execute again tomorrow.

Acknowledge your progress, learn from the missteps, and never stop moving forward.

The ones who refuse to quit are the ones who ultimately win.
