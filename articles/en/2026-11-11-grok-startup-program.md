SpaceAI just gave early-stage founders a pretty good reason to look at Grok.

The new Startup Program offers up to 12 months of free credits across Grok, Grok Build, Grok Bot and the Grok API. For teams already spending a few thousand per month on AI tools, that can make a real difference to runway.

The allocation depends on funding, revenue and current AI spend. Part of the credits is released after approval, with the rest following onboarding.

What you can actually use it for:

- Grok Build for coding and shipping
- Grok Bot for workflow automation
- Grok API inside your product
- Grok for research and drafting

You also get onboarding support and access to the startup relations team if usage grows.

The main catch: it is aimed at VC-backed startups with a live product and a proper company domain.

If Grok fits your stack, this is worth checking before paying full infrastructure costs out of pocket.
