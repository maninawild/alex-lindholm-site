Searching for users and investors across the US, UK, EU, or GCC can quickly become a full-time job. Fortunately, the modern software stack can handle the heavy lifting.

Here is the exact tech stack to streamline your pipeline.

## Find the right buyers

- clay.com: Automatically surfaces companies that just closed a round, are actively hiring, or underwent leadership changes.
- trigify.io: Converts LinkedIn post engagement into warm, actionable leads.
- sparktoro.com: Reveals which podcasts, newsletters, and sites your ideal buyers follow.
- apollo.io: Delivers verified contact data and email lists.
- dealfront.com: Tracks company website visits with GDPR compliance for the UK and EU.

## Outreach

- lemlist.com: Combines email and LinkedIn touchpoints into a unified sequence.
- loom.com: Cuts through busy executive inboxes with concise, personalized video walkthroughs.

## Omnichannel presence

- typefully.com: Publishes a single draft across LinkedIn, X, and other channels.
- opus.pro: Turns long calls and podcasts into short-form clips.
- luma.com: Simplifies hosting founder events and managing your community list.

## Pipeline tracking

- attio.com: Acts as a flexible CRM for customers and investors.
- fathom.video: Records and transcribes calls so you never miss what buyers care about.

## Fundraising

- signal.nfx.com: A free investor database filterable by stage and sector.
- openvc.app: Streamlines targeted investor searches.
- dealroom.co: Provides startup and investor data across Europe.
- magnitt.com: Tracks investor and funding activity across the Middle East.
- gamma.app: Builds clean investor decks and one-pagers.
- papermark.com: Tracks slide-by-slide engagement when you share decks with VCs.
- visible.vc: Streamlines investor update emails.

## Non-dilutive capital many founders miss

- US: sbir.gov
- UK: Innovate UK grants — https://lnkd.in/ebdWBcQ2
- EU: eic.ec.europa.eu
- GCC: hub71.com

No software tool can fix a weak narrative or unclear positioning. But used right, they will save your team dozens of hours every week.
