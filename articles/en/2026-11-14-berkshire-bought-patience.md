Berkshire Hathaway is sitting on $365 billion in cash.

Insane: it’s enough to buy 472 companies from the S&P 500. Six months ago the pile was even bigger: around $397 billion.

And now, under Greg Abel, Berkshire finally seems to be moving again.

Q2 reportedly brought:

- around $20B in net stock purchases
- roughly $10B into Alphabet
- about $4.5B in buybacks
- around $6.8B committed to Taylor Morrison

Buffett spent years looking at expensive markets and basically saying: no, thanks.

Now Berkshire has one of the biggest cash piles in history just as the AI trade starts getting repriced.

Not a bad moment to have dry powder.

Everyone else spent the last few years buying GPUs.

Berkshire bought patience.
